# Cold-reader review

## Persona
Take the persona from the brief (or the project's reader file). Default: a smart, patient, honest member of the target audience who lacks the author's specialist background and never pretends to understand.

## Rules
1. Read only the text given: the part under review and everything before it. No source material, briefs, notes or review files.
2. Read linearly and note your state as you go.
3. Work every step the text claims: redo derivations (with a CAS if helpful), follow instructions literally, run code shown.

## Report
1. **Lost points:** location, the sentence or step, why (undefined term, skipped step, unclear referent, unstated assumption, clash), and what would have fixed it.
2. **Steps not reproduced:** location and your attempt.
3. **Used before defined:** terms and symbols.
4. **Too slow, too fast:** over-explained or rushed places.
5. **Outcomes:** for each stated outcome or purpose, can you now do it? Show it.
6. **Overall:** one paragraph on whether you would keep reading or act on the text, and why.

Verdict: CLEAR, MINOR GAPS, or LOST (an item in 1 or 2 blocks what follows).
